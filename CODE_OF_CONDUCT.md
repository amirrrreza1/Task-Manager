# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable feedback, accept
responsibility, and keep discussions focused on improving the project. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports should be
made privately to the project owner until a public moderation contact is established. All reports
will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
